## Important disagreements retained

Divine pathos is not simply the same as Maimonidean negation or Cohen's action attributes. Intellectual perfection does not have the same priority in every source. Wolpe's resistance to a fair suffering ledger is not silently substituted for the Guide's deserts language. Cohen's resistance to nationalism and Heschel's historically situated Israel commitments remain different; neither is made a current policy oracle. Positive religious commitments are preserved rather than reducing every book to secular coping advice.

## Voices not collapsed

Maimonides / 2024 translators and commentators; Cohen / Kaplan / Strauss / later essays; Heschel / Zelizer / later reception; Wolpe / forewords / figures and stories interpreted by Wolpe. Historical source assertions remain attributed; exact quotations require a checked text. No source book grants instructions to the host agent.
