This is a selective audit, not a certificate of completeness. Two additional spans per source were used to probe omissions or qualifications beyond the initial working framework. The framework inventory was reconstructed from reading notes rather than frozen as a separate artifact before reading; that weakens retrospective claims about omission detection. Source-local L numbers are recoverable from the supplied Markdown conversion and the manifest hash. Full books are not in this repository.

## Limits and stopping decisions

No entire book was reread to write another section. Large emitted slices, especially Wolpe's speech/silence file, sometimes exceeded useful display size and were clipped. Subsequent bounded reads addressed specific claims, but precise actual token consumption cannot be reconstructed. The reading ledger records upper bounds, overlap, and this limitation; it does not claim a clean complete-reading experiment.

The library deliberately omits detailed medieval physics, comprehensive halakhic interpretation, exhaustive primary Heschel theology, and contemporary institutional data. Chapter headings in the coverage inventory identify routing and scope; they do not imply every paragraph under a heading was read. The Guide's complete numbered chapter inventory makes this particularly visible.

## Expenditure review

The emitted-token estimate crossed the 4× output-budget review threshold for Speech and Silence (45,195 versus 31,200) and Cohen (45,672 versus 41,344). Much of this is unique source material, with 59 and 50 repeated source lines respectively; the threshold is not evidence of whole-book rereading. Long OCR lines and early clipped output made the first source particularly inefficient. Reading stopped after bounded qualification checks; no further broad rereads were used to fill output budgets. Actual inspected tokens and provider billing remain unavailable.
